# Hedwig direction

Hedwig focuses on a unified inbox with context, explainable sorting, and a user-controlled assistant. The [README](README.md) describes implemented features; [Team-AER/hedwig issues](https://github.com/Team-AER/hedwig/issues) are the place to discuss bugs, priorities, and proposals.

## Current product areas

- People, Reading, and Records streams, Screener, sorting reasons, corrections, and undo.
- Reply Later, Set Aside, Snoozed, Waiting on, Bills, Ledger, and Daily Brief.
- Search and cited answers across mail, people/topic context, and commitments.
- Approved agent actions, read-only scheduled automations, and permissioned plugins.
- Desktop pane layouts, phone navigation, and accessible keyboard workflows.

## Plans and historical evidence

[The v2 product PRD](docs/hedwig/PRD-v2-product.md), [AI rebuild PRD](docs/hedwig/PRD-ai-rebuild.md), [v2 build plan](docs/hedwig/V2-BUILD.md), and [design build notes](docs/hedwig/design/REDESIGN-BUILD-2026-09-24.md) preserve product intent and implementation history. They are not commitments or proof that every described behavior is released. Dated audit documents likewise describe the state observed at the time.

Future work should improve mail reliability, evidence quality, correction workflows, and the plugin trust boundary. Propose concrete work through this fork’s issue tracker; no release dates are promised here.

With thanks to [MailFlow](https://github.com/maathimself/mailflow) for the foundation. Its roadmap belongs to the upstream project and does not define Hedwig’s release plan.
